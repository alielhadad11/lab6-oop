SkillForge System is the system boundary where all operations take place. Student is the user who uses the platform and can perform many operations. First, he browses courses to view available courses. Then, he enrolls in course to join the course he chose, and this must happen after browsing courses. He also completes lesson to finish the lessons. And he takes quiz to answer the tests. Finally, he views certificate to see his certificate. And the certificate is generated automatically when he finishes the quiz.

Instructor is the teacher and can perform certain operations. He creates course to create a new course. And he uploads lesson to upload lessons and content. And he designs quiz to create test questions. And he views analytics to see statistics of his students.

Admin is the platform controller and can perform important operations. He manages users to add or delete users. And he approves course to approve new courses. And he removes course to delete courses. And he monitors analytics to see statistics of the entire platform.

The dashed lines show that Browse Courses must happen before Enroll in Course, and that Generate Certificate is automatically generated after Take Quiz.
